How this tool calculates

  1. Select one score, 1–5, for each task.
  2. That score sets a maximum minutes per day.
  3. Enter the actual minutes for each day of the week.
  4. Weekly minutes = sum of the seven days × tasks per day.

Scores 1–2 route to supervision (ECLS). Scores 3–5 route to personal care (PCS). One billing unit is 15 minutes.
